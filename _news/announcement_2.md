I was selected for the [ICTS S. N. Bhatt Memorial Excellence Fellowship Program 2026](https://www.icts.res.in/academic/summer-research-program/2026). My selection is listed in the [official ICTS results](https://www.icts.res.in/news/results-icts-s-n-bhatt-memorial-excellence-fellowship-program-2026).

During this fellowship, I worked with Prof. Abhishek Dhar on quantum open systems, with a focus on nonequilibrium dynamics, decoherence, and system-environment interactions.
